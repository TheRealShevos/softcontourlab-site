What you're seeing

Every voxel gets a tumour probability. The solid line is where that probability crosses 50%. The glowing band shows where the model is less sure, which is exactly where a radiologist's attention should go.

What's under the hood

The MVP runs an open-source baseline model through our soft-contour pipeline: CT only, no report needed. Our own model replaces it as training matures.

What's next

Upload your own scan, results inside your existing viewer, and per-scan pricing for pilot partners. Talk to us about joining the pilot.
