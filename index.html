Why the edge

Finding a tumour isn't the same as outlining it.

Pancreatic cancer is infiltrative: it wraps vessels and often extends past the gland. A useful outline has to show where it really ends.

Most pipelines approximate the tumour's shape, which works for finding it. Then they cut away anything outside the pancreas, which removes real tumour at exactly the edge that matters.
