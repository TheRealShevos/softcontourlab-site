Privacy policy.

Short version: this website doesn't track you, set cookies or collect personal information through forms. If you email us, we use your details only to reply.

Last updated 23 September 2026

Who we are

Soft Contour Lab (“we”, “us”) is an early-stage startup based in Sydney, Australia, building software that highlights tumour boundaries on CT scans. This policy covers softcontourlab.me.

What we collect

Through this website: nothing directly. There are no forms, accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking cookies.

When you email us: your name, email address and whatever you choose to include in your message.

Third parties that see technical data

  • GitHub Pages hosts this site. Like any web host, it receives your IP address and browser details when you load a page, and may keep them in server logs under GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Google Fonts serves the site's typefaces, so your browser requests font files from Google. See Google Fonts' privacy FAQ.

How we use your information

Only to reply to you and to follow up on the conversation you started. We don't sell or rent personal information, and we don't add you to a mailing list without asking.

Health information

Please don't send us patient data, medical images or health records. This website and our email are not set up to receive them. If something like that reaches us by mistake, we'll delete it and let you know.

Keeping and deleting information

We keep email correspondence only as long as we need it for the conversation. Ask us at any time to see, correct or delete what we hold about you.

Your rights

We aim to handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles. If you're concerned about how we've handled your information, contact us first. You can also contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
